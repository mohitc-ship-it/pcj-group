# AI-Powered Tech Pack Automation System

**Prepared for PCJ Group**
**Prepared by ConsultADD | September 2026**

## Overview

An AI-powered system that generates complete 9-page Technical Package PDFs from garment photographs and minimal context. The system will be trained on PCJ Group's existing tech packs to learn your team's specific style of tech pack creation — enabling it to produce factory-oriented outputs that match your standards and can be applied to new, unique designs.

**Goal:** Reduce tech pack creation time from 5–6 hours per style to a 20–30 minute expert verification task.

---

## How It Works

**Input:** 2 garment photos (front + back) + brand / collection / season / fabric preference / size range

**Output:** A 9-page tech pack PDF:

| Page | Content |
|------|---------|
| Cover | Garment images, brand, collection, auto-generated style code, logo |
| 3D CAD Design | Front/back views, detail close-ups, color swatch with Pantone TCX, garment description |
| Technical Sketch | AI-generated line drawings with labeled construction callouts |
| Accessories | Thread, labels, interlining — quantities, dimensions, placement |
| Product Construction | Seam types, stitch codes, seam allowances, machine types |
| Measurements | Measurement diagram, points of measurement with tolerances |
| Fabric & Quality Standards | Composition, GSM, ISO test requirements, QC notes |
| Size Chart | Graded measurements across full size range |
| Wash & Care | Care instructions, composition, care label image, compliance standards |

The system classifies the garment type automatically from images — dresses, coats, jackets, trousers, tops, skirts, outerwear — and tailors every page's content to the identified garment.

## Training & Accuracy

The system will be trained on PCJ Group's existing tech packs during the development period. This training biases the AI toward your team's conventions — your measurement standards, your construction terminology, your fabric preferences, your template structure.

**Accuracy approach:**

| Phase | What Happens | Expected Accuracy |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| Weeks 1–3 | Core pipeline build + training on PCJ tech packs | ~80% |
| Weeks 4–5 | Expert feedback from Vidisha's team, prompt refinement, cross-field verification | ~88-90% |
| Week 6 | Final testing, edge case handling, acceptance validation | ~90% |
| Post-delivery (ongoing) | Continued expert feedback + correction cycles | 90% → 95%+ over time |

**What accuracy means practically:** At ~90%, your technical designer opens the generated tech pack and makes minor corrections — adjusting a measurement value, refining a construction note — rather than building the entire document from scratch. The expert review step (20–30 minutes) is part of the workflow, not a limitation.

**The path to 95–98%:** Accuracy improves continuously as your experts use the tool. Each correction teaches the system. Over weeks and months of real usage, the AI adapts to PCJ's specific garment types, preferred terminology, and factory requirements. ConsultADD will support this training process during and after the engagement.
